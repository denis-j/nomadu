## Description (4000)

Nomadu keeps count of where you have been, so you know how long you can stay. It logs every country and city on its own, then tracks your Schengen days, visa-free stays and the days that count toward tax residency.

Built for digital nomads, remote workers and long-term travelers who move every few weeks and are tired of keeping a spreadsheet.

KNOW YOUR VISA DAYS
- Schengen 90/180 calculator that rolls with every day you spend in the area
- Visa-free allowances for your passport in 199 countries
- Add your own visas: digital nomad visas, e-visas, per-stay or rolling limits
- Alerts at 75%, 90% and 100% of a limit, and a reminder before a visa expires

STAY AHEAD OF TAX RESIDENCY
- Days per country, counted in each country's own tax year
- Handles the UK, Australian and New Zealand tax years, not just January to December
- See how close you are to the 183-day line before you book the next flight

YOUR TRAVEL LOG, WITHOUT THE WORK
- Records your stays in the background, battery friendly
- A timeline and a map of every city you have lived in
- Add past trips by hand, or import them from screenshots of your bookings
- Days away from home, countries, cities and average stay at a glance

PLAN THE NEXT TRIP
- Add stops and dates, and see the visa days you would have left at each one
- AI ideas for where to go next on your route
- Compare places to stay side by side: price per night, rating, cancellation
- Keep tickets, passports and booking confirmations with the trip

TRAVEL TOGETHER
- Share a trip with a link
- Let friends add stops and places to stay, or keep it view only. You decide per person.

COLLECT EVERY COUNTRY
- Earn a 3D badge for each new country you live in

FOR PEOPLE WHO AUTOMATE
- Connect your AI agent: it can read your days and add stops for you

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No ads. Your data is never sold.
- Delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Nomadu gives estimates to help you plan. It is not legal or tax advice. Rules change and depend on your situation, so check with the official source or an advisor before you decide.
